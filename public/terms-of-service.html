1. Overview

At Love U Convert, the security and privacy of your data are at the heart of everything we do. Our systems are built with bank-grade encryption, ISO 27001-certified cloud infrastructure, and global privacy compliance to ensure every file conversion is protected from unauthorized access, misuse, or data leaks.

We continuously review and update our policies and systems to comply with international data protection laws — including:

  • EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
  • India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023
  • Other applicable regional privacy and cybersecurity frameworks

Our mission is simple: to keep your data private, secure, and temporary.

2. Infrastructure Security

All file conversions and data processing are handled on secure, ISO 27001-certified cloud servers.

Key Measures Include:

  • Encrypted File Transfers: Every file upload and download is protected using 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption, ensuring your data is never exposed during transmission.
  • Isolated Processing Environments: Each file conversion is executed within a temporary, sandboxed container, preventing cross-access between users or sessions.
  • Automatic File Deletion: All uploaded and converted files are automatically deleted after 2 hours, leaving no trace on our servers. We do not maintain manual logs, file names, or content history.
  • Limited Data Retention: IP addresses are stored temporarily (for 24 hours) only to enforce fair-use rate limiting — and are automatically cleared thereafter.

3. Organizational Security

Our internal processes are designed to maintain strict data discipline.

Access Control

  • Only authorized technical personnel can access system-level components.
  • No staff or contractor has permission to view, copy, or retrieve user-uploaded content.
  • All staff members undergo privacy and security training aligned with global data protection regulations.

Change Management & Monitoring

  • Continuous infrastructure monitoring to detect unauthorized access or anomalies.
  • Regular security audits and patch updates are conducted on all systems.

4. Data Encryption Standards

All user data handled through Love U Convert follows strict encryption practices:

Phase Encryption Type Purpose
File Upload/Download 256-bit SSL/TLS Secure transmission
Temporary Storage AES-256 Data protection during processing
API Communication HTTPS + Token-based Auth Secure server interaction

Our encryption standards meet and exceed modern cybersecurity requirements for data in transit and at rest.

5. Compliance with International Privacy Laws

a) GDPR (European Union)

We comply with all core GDPR principles:

  • Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency
  • Purpose limitation (files are used only for conversion)
  • Data minimization & storage limitation
  • Integrity and confidentiality

Users in the EU have full rights to:

  • Access, rectify, or delete any personal data we hold (if any)
  • Request data portability or restriction of processing
  • File complaints with their regional Data Protection Authority

b) CCPA (California, USA)

In compliance with the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • We do not sell or rent user data.
  • We do not collect personal identifiers beyond temporary IP storage for rate limiting.
  • Users can contact us to request confirmation that no personal data is retained.

c) DPDP Act (India)

As an India-based company, Love U Convert adheres to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023):

  • Ensures lawful and fair processing of digital data.
  • Protects data principals' (users') rights to privacy and consent.
  • Implements strong security safeguards against unauthorized access or misuse.

6. Google Ads & Analytics Compliance

We use Google AdSense and Google Analytics to generate revenue and improve user experience. These third-party tools may use cookies to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads based on Google's advertising policies.

Key Assurances:

  • We do not sell, rent, or share user data for ad targeting.
  • Google Ads data is processed under Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Users can manage their ad preferences or opt out through Google's Ad Settings.

7. Incident Response & Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data incident:

  • A dedicated response team will investigate and contain the breach immediately.
  • Affected users and regulatory authorities (as applicable under GDPR or DPDP Act) will be notified promptly.
  • Full forensic analysis and security remediation will follow to prevent recurrence.

8. API & Data Handling Policy

Love U Convert may use third-party APIs to perform file conversions and processing tasks. All such APIs are verified for:

  • ISO 27001 certification
  • Data protection compliance
  • Limited use and secure data transmission

Our use and transfer of data from Google APIs strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements. We do not share, sell, or disclose API-derived data to any third-party service.

9. User Rights & Control

Even though Love U Convert does not require user accounts or collect identifiable information, we still uphold your fundamental privacy rights:

  • Right to Know what data (if any) is temporarily processed.
  • Right to Erasure (files deleted automatically after 2 hours).
  • Right to Non-Discrimination (we provide the same service regardless of data preferences).

To exercise your rights or ask privacy-related questions, contact: 📧 Contact@love-u-convert.com

10. Continuous Compliance & Review

We regularly review our:

  • Infrastructure security policies
  • Data handling practices
  • Legal compliance measures

All systems are continuously monitored and updated to reflect the latest cybersecurity standards, regulations, and best practices.
