About Us

Welcome to Love U Convert — Your Trusted Online File Conversion Platform

At Love U Convert, we believe file conversion should be simple, secure, and lightning-fast. Our platform enables users worldwide to convert their files across multiple formats — safely, privately, and without creating an account.

Whether you're a student, professional, or casual user, Love U Convert provides a clean, ad-supported, and privacy-focused experience. Every conversion is powered by ISO 27001-certified cloud infrastructure, ensuring bank-grade data security throughout the process.

Our Mission

To make digital file conversion effortless and accessible to everyone — while protecting user privacy and data integrity at every step. We aim to deliver a transparent, secure, and eco-efficient conversion environment without compromising quality or trust.

What Makes Us Different

1. Privacy-First Approach

  • We never require sign-ups or accounts.
  • No files or personal data are permanently stored.
  • All uploads and outputs are automatically deleted after 2 hours.

2. Global-Standard Security

  • Files are processed exclusively on ISO 27001-certified cloud servers.
  • Transfers use 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption to ensure privacy and integrity.

3. Transparent Revenue Model

  • Our services are 100 % free to use, supported only by Google Ads.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share user data with any third party.

4. No Hidden Fees, No Subscriptions

  • Love U Convert runs on fairness and simplicity — no registration, no hidden charges.
  • You only interact with your file — safely and quickly.

5. Compliant with Global Laws

  • Fully aligned with GDPR, CCPA, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023).
  • Regularly reviewed for legal compliance and data protection updates.

Our Technology & Infrastructure

Love U Convert integrates advanced file-processing APIs with secure cloud automation. Each upload, conversion, and download passes through a temporary, isolated environment to ensure total privacy. We employ automated deletion protocols, encrypted transmission layers, and rate-limiting (2 GB per IP per 24 hours) to maintain performance and fairness.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted, privacy-driven file conversion platform, empowering individuals and businesses alike to handle files securely across devices and borders — without compromising convenience or compliance.

Commitment to Transparency

We understand that trust must be earned. That's why every policy — from our Privacy Policy to our Terms of Service — is crafted with full clarity and legal transparency. Users are encouraged to review these documents anytime to understand exactly how we handle data and maintain integrity.
